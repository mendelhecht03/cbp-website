Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Anshei Moshe Chabad Inc., operating as Chabad of Boro Park ("we," "us," "our"), respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or make a donation, and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you make a donation, we collect the information needed to process your contribution, which may include your name, email address, mailing address, phone number, and payment details. Payment card information is processed directly by our payment provider, Stripe, and is not stored on our servers.

When you contact us by phone or email, we receive the contact information you provide.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process donations and issue tax receipts
  • Respond to inquiries and communicate with donors
  • Maintain records required for nonprofit reporting and compliance

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share information only with service providers necessary to operate (such as Stripe for payment processing) or when required by law.

Data Retention

We retain donor and contact records as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described above and to comply with legal and tax obligations.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us using the details below. We will honor such requests except where retention is required by law.
